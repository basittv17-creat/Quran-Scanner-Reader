Quran Scan Reader — Offline audio assets (Phase 7)

No Quran recitation audio is bundled with this source distribution.

To play a verse, a user must import their own lawfully licensed, independently reviewed
ayah-by-ayah MP3 ZIP through the Offline Recitation screen. The archive MUST contain
manifest.tsv and the exact files listed there, with SHA-256 digests.

The importer's structural/hash checks do NOT establish accurate recitation, tajweed,
identity of the reciter, or permission to redistribute audio. Do not label any pack
verified without independent source/rights and verse-by-verse review.

The full canonical Quran text corpus is NOT yet bundled. While it is absent, playback
is intentionally disabled even if a user imports an audio pack.

See docs/PHASE7_AUDIO_PACK_FORMAT.md.
